### Indigenous Data Governance in Health (POPH90308)

**University of Melbourne · Graduate subject · Semester 2 (On Campus and Online)**

This subject provides practical understandings of the production and strategic dissemination of Indigenous health data central to governance and research in Indigenous public health. Topics include data governance, ethical health research, knowledge translation, decolonising methodologies, and the development of ethical and collaborative community partnerships.
