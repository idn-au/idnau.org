     Our mission
    ::
 Our mission is to improve how Indigenous data is collected, managed and used over time. We do this by supporting access, governance and shared responsibility across research, policy and community settings, while building the capability of Indigenous data stewards and custodians across Australia in all sectors
 <br/>
 <br/>
 Built by the Indigenous Data Network, the Indigenous Data Commons provides a trusted national space where Indigenous data are governed by Indigenous owners and used for Indigenous benefit. Through our online tools, we aim to establish an ethical, Indigenous-led approach to data governance across research, policy, and community settings in Australia.
