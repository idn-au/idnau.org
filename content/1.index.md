# The Indigenous Data Commons

Greater access to data and information is critical for Indigenous community organisations. The Indigenous Data Commons is a digital initiative and comprehensive online platform developed by the [Indigenous Data Network](https://mspgh.unimelb.edu.au/centres-institutes/onemda/research-group/indigenous-studies-unit/indigenous-data-network) (IDN). The online platform supports and empowers Indigenous data custodians and stewards, to create and augment their data sources, and to discover Indigenous data created and held by other organisations, institutions, government agencies or people.
